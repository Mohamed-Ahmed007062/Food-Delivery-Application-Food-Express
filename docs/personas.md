# User Personas

This document outlines the primary user personas for the Food Delivery Web Application. Understanding these personas helps ensure the product meets the specific needs, goals, and expectations of its diverse user base.

---

## 🧑‍💻 Persona 1: Customer (Primary)

**Name:** Ahmed Al-Mansouri  
**Age:** 28  
**Occupation:** Marketing Manager  
**Location:** Dubai, UAE  

### 📝 Bio
Ahmed is a busy young professional who works long hours and frequently relies on food delivery for lunch at the office and dinner at home. He values convenience, speed, and variety. He enjoys exploring new cuisines but also has a few go-to restaurants that he orders from regularly. 

### 🎯 Goals
- **Quick Ordering:** Wants to order food with minimal clicks, especially for repeat orders.
- **Real-Time Tracking:** Needs to know exactly when his food will arrive so he can manage his breaks.
- **Discovery:** Wants personalized recommendations to find new, highly-rated restaurants.
- **Favorites:** Easily save and reorder from his favorite restaurants.
- **Convenience:** Expects seamless payment options (Apple Pay, Credit Card) and saved delivery addresses.

### 😫 Pain Points
- Frustrated by apps that crash or lag during checkout.
- Hates inaccurate delivery time estimates and lack of driver communication.
- Overwhelmed by cluttered interfaces that make finding specific meals difficult.
- Dislikes hidden fees that only appear at the final checkout step.

### 💻 Tech Profile
- **Tech Savviness:** High
- **Devices:** iPhone 14 Pro, MacBook Air
- **Usage Frequency:** 3-5 times/week

> **Key Quote:** *"After a long day of meetings, I just want to order my favorite meal in three taps and know exactly when it will reach my door."*

## 👩‍🍳 Persona 2: Restaurant Owner

**Name:** Fatima Hassan  
**Age:** 42  
**Occupation:** Owner & Manager of "Levantine Delights"  
**Location:** Cairo, Egypt  

### 📝 Bio
Fatima runs a popular local Middle Eastern restaurant. While she excels at hospitality and culinary arts, she is trying to expand her business's digital footprint. She relies on the delivery platform to bring in new customers and increase daily order volume without overwhelming her kitchen staff.

### 🎯 Goals
- **Menu Management:** Easily add new seasonal dishes, update prices, and mark items as "sold out."
- **Efficient Order Processing:** Receive clear, immediate notifications for incoming orders to maintain kitchen flow.
- **Revenue Tracking:** Access simple, clear reports on daily/weekly earnings and popular items.
- **Business Growth:** Respond to customer reviews and run promotional discounts to attract more diners.

### 😫 Pain Points
- Complex, difficult-to-navigate merchant dashboards that require extensive training.
- Delayed order notifications leading to slow preparation and unhappy customers.
- Poor analytics that don't clearly explain which menu items are performing best.
- Unfair dispute resolutions regarding canceled orders.

### 💻 Tech Profile
- **Tech Savviness:** Medium
- **Devices:** Windows Desktop (in the back office), iPad (at the host stand)
- **Usage Frequency:** Daily (Continuous monitoring during operating hours)

> **Key Quote:** *"I need a system that works in the background seamlessly, so my staff can focus on cooking great food, not fighting with a tablet."*

## 👨‍💼 Persona 3: Admin

**Name:** Omar Khalil  
**Age:** 35  
**Occupation:** Platform Operations Administrator  
**Location:** Remote (Headquarters)  

### 📝 Bio
Omar is responsible for the day-to-day operations and health of the food delivery platform. He monitors system stability, onboards new restaurant partners, handles escalated customer disputes, and ensures that the marketplace remains balanced and profitable.

### 🎯 Goals
- **Platform Health:** Monitor active orders, system uptime, and overall performance in real-time.
- **Entity Management:** Efficiently approve, suspend, or edit user and restaurant accounts.
- **Data Analytics:** View high-level metrics like Gross Merchandise Value (GMV), active users, and revenue trends.
- **Dispute Resolution:** Quickly access transaction histories to resolve customer or restaurant complaints.

### 😫 Pain Points
- Lack of real-time dashboards forces him to refresh pages to see current platform status.
- Manual, tedious processes for onboarding restaurants or verifying documents.
- Scattered data that requires exporting multiple CSVs to generate a single report.
- Inability to quickly apply platform-wide coupons or broadcast emergency banners.

### 💻 Tech Profile
- **Tech Savviness:** High (Comfortable with databases, API logs, and advanced dashboards)
- **Devices:** Dual-monitor Windows Desktop setup
- **Usage Frequency:** Daily (Full-time job)

> **Key Quote:** *"Data is everything. If I can't see a live overhead view of the marketplace, I can't fix problems before they affect our users."*
